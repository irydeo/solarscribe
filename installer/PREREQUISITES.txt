SolarScribe 0.1: what this computer needs
=========================================

Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit.

CAMERA
  The camera SDKs that may legally be redistributed travel inside this
  installer. If your camera model needs a vendor SDK that is not included,
  SolarScribe says which one is missing and keeps working with the simulator
  and with any other camera it finds. Install the camera vendor's driver or
  SDK, then reopen SolarScribe.

  The per-SDK licence notes are in the "licenses" folder next to the
  application.

MOUNT: ASCOM PLATFORM
  The Windows mount backend uses ASCOM COM. It needs:
    - ASCOM Platform 7 (or the current version), and
    - the .NET runtime that the ASCOM Platform installer asks for.
  Neither is bundled: they are separate installations from ascom-standards.org.
  The Python COM bridge (pywin32) IS included in this build.

  Without the ASCOM Platform the camera, the SHG engine and the session flow
  still work; the telescope panel reports that the mount backend is missing
  and the mount simulator can be used for a dry run.

INTERNET
  Optional. SolarScribe is local by design; the network is used only for the
  optional SDO Sun image. The ephemeris and the Sun position are computed
  locally.

No administrator rights are needed: the installer writes to
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SolarScribe, and your settings and sessions stay in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\SolarScribe.
